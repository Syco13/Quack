Features

Speech-aware ducking

Silero VAD detects when you speak and triggers ducking automatically.

Spotify session control

Targets Spotify’s app-session volume using Windows Core Audio.

Configurable feel

Set your duck level and release delay to match your setup.

Tray-first workflow

Runs from the tray and stays out of your way while you work or game.

Windows-first

Built for Windows 10/11 and tuned for local desktop reliability.

Private + open source

Runs locally on your machine, with source available under MIT.

How it works

  1. Listen: Quack monitors your selected microphone.
  2. Duck: When speech is detected, Spotify volume is lowered.
  3. Restore: Volume returns after your configured release delay.

Requirements & compatibility

  • Windows 10 or Windows 11
  • Spotify (Free or Premium)
  • A working microphone
  • Currently Windows-only

FAQ

Do I need OBS?

No. Quack is a standalone Windows desktop app.

Does it work with Spotify Free and Premium?

Yes, as long as Spotify is running on Windows.

Does Quack send my microphone data anywhere?

No cloud requirement is documented. Quack operates locally on your machine.

Why can first launch feel slow?

Windows may take 10–30 seconds on first run while unpacking the installed app.

What operating systems are supported?

Windows 10 and Windows 11 only for now.
